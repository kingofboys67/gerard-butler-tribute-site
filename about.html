Scottish actor, producer and former law graduate who became one of Hollywood's most recognisable leading men. // FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

GERARD BUTLER FAQ

Key answers to the most searched questions about Gerard Butler's life, career and philanthropy.

What is Gerard Butler's full name and where was he born?
Gerard James Butler was born on 13 November 1969 in Paisley, Renfrewshire, Scotland. His family moved to Montreal, Canada, when he was a baby; after his parents separated he returned to Scotland as a young child and was raised in Paisley by his mother.
How old is Gerard Butler?
Born on 13 November 1969, Gerard Butler turned 57 in November 2026.
Did Gerard Butler practise law before becoming an actor?
He earned a law degree from the University of Glasgow, served as President of the university's Law Society, and trained at an Edinburgh law firm. He was let go shortly before he would have qualified, and turned to acting full time.
What was Gerard Butler's first film?
His screen debut was in Mrs Brown (1997), followed that year by a small role in the James Bond film Tomorrow Never Dies.
What was Gerard Butler's breakthrough role?
He became an international star playing King Leonidas in Zack Snyder's 300 (2006), following his lead role in The Phantom of the Opera (2004).
How did Gerard Butler train for 300?
He trained for around four months with coach Mark Twight, combining weightlifting, kettlebell circuits and sword-and-shield choreography.
What movies are in the Has Fallen series?
Butler plays Secret Service agent Mike Banning in Olympus Has Fallen (2013), London Has Fallen (2016) and Angel Has Fallen (2019).
Does Gerard Butler voice Stoick the Vast in How to Train Your Dragon?
Yes. He voiced Stoick across the DreamWorks animated trilogy (2010, 2014, 2019) and reprised the role on screen in the 2025 live-action film directed by Dean DeBlois.
What is G-BASE and who co-founded it?
G-BASE is the production company Butler co-founded in 2008 with his manager Alan Siegel. Its credits include Law Abiding Citizen, Olympus Has Fallen, Greenland, Plane and Greenland 2: Migration.
Is Gerard Butler married?
Gerard Butler is not married and has no publicly known children. He has consistently kept his private life out of the press.
How tall is Gerard Butler?
He is widely reported to be 6 ft 2 in (1.88 m) tall.
What charities does Gerard Butler support?
He is a long-standing Global Ambassador for Mary's Meals, which provides school meals to more than 2.4 million children, and a supporter of Direct Relief.
Did Gerard Butler have a surfing accident while filming?
Yes. In December 2011, while filming Chasing Mavericks at Mavericks in Northern California, he was held under by a set of large waves and was taken to hospital as a precaution.
What awards has Gerard Butler won?
He won the MTV Movie Award for Best Fight (2007) for 300, a Taurus World Stunt Award (2007), and Germany's Golden Camera for Best International Actor (2016). See the full table under Quick Facts, Stats & Awards.
// THE FULL STORY

Biography & Career Journey

Chapter 01

Early Life & Roots

Gerard James Butler was born in Paisley, Renfrewshire, on 13 November 1969, the youngest of three children of Margaret and Edward Butler. When he was still a baby the family moved to Montreal, Canada; after his parents separated, his mother brought the children home to Scotland, and Gerard grew up in Paisley. He would not see his father again until he was 16.

A bright, restless student, he joined the Scottish Youth Theatre as a boy but chose the safe road at university, studying law at the University of Glasgow, where he became President of the Law Society. He went on to a traineeship at an Edinburgh firm — and was let go shortly before he would have qualified as a solicitor.

It turned out to be the push he needed. At 25 he moved to London, determined to act, and took whatever work he could find while knocking on doors.

Chapter 02

Breakthrough & Mid-Career

His break came on stage: a chance meeting with actor-director Steven Berkoff led to a role in Coriolanus, followed by the stage adaptation of Trainspotting. On screen he debuted in Mrs Brown (1997) opposite Judi Dench and Billy Connolly, appeared in the Bond film Tomorrow Never Dies, then led Dracula 2000, the miniseries Attila and Reign of Fire.

In 2004 he surprised critics by singing the title role in Joel Schumacher's The Phantom of the Opera. Two years later Zack Snyder's 300 made him a global star: his King Leonidas — and the line “This is Sparta!” — became part of pop culture, and the film grossed around $456 million worldwide.

He then showed his range with romantic leads in P.S. I Love You (2007) and The Ugly Truth (2009), and began voicing the Viking chief Stoick the Vast in DreamWorks' How to Train Your Dragon (2010).

Chapter 03

Blockbusters & Producing

In 2008 Butler co-founded G-BASE with his manager Alan Siegel to take more control of his work. Its first major release, Law Abiding Citizen (2009), saw him produce and star as the vengeful Clyde Shelton.

Olympus Has Fallen (2013) introduced Secret Service agent Mike Banning and grew into a trilogy with London Has Fallen (2016) and Angel Has Fallen (2019). He followed with the heist thriller Den of Thieves (2018) as Big Nick O'Brien, the submarine drama Hunter Killer (2018), and the disaster film Greenland (2020).

The 2020s brought Copshop, Last Seen Alive, Plane (2023) and Kandahar (2023), with G-BASE increasingly behind the projects he leads — making him one of the few actors who reliably carries mid-budget action films both on screen and as a producer.

Chapter 04

Who He Is Today

Away from the camera, Butler is a long-standing Global Ambassador for Mary's Meals, the Scottish charity that provides school meals to more than 2.4 million children, and he has visited its programmes in person. He also supports disaster relief through Direct Relief.

He has spoken openly about resilience — from losing his legal career to surviving a frightening wipeout while filming Chasing Mavericks in 2011 — and about staying grounded in his Scottish roots.

In 2025 he returned as Stoick in the live-action How to Train Your Dragon and as Big Nick in Den of Thieves 2: Pantera, and in January 2026 he led and produced Greenland 2: Migration. More than 25 years after Mrs Brown, he remains a box-office draw with a career spanning drama, musical, comedy, animation and action.
