// QUICK ANSWERS

Where to watch: quick answers

Where can I watch Gerard Butler movies?

Most Gerard Butler films are available to rent or buy on Apple TV, Amazon Prime Video and Google Play, and several rotate through Netflix, Prime Video and Paramount+. Availability changes by country and month, so check the live listings for your region.

Is 300 streaming?

300 (2006) is generally available to rent or buy digitally and rotates on subscription services in most regions. Use the streaming check on this page for live availability where you are.

What order should I watch the Has Fallen films in?

Olympus Has Fallen (2013), London Has Fallen (2016), Angel Has Fallen (2019) and then Night Has Fallen, which is in post-production.

Where can I watch Greenland 2: Migration?

Greenland 2: Migration was released in cinemas on 9 January 2026 by Lionsgate, followed by home entertainment and streaming releases. Check the listings for your region.
