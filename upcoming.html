// QUICK ANSWERS

Upcoming projects: quick answers

Is Greenland 2 out?

Yes. Greenland 2: Migration was released in cinemas on 9 January 2026 by Lionsgate, with Gerard Butler returning as John Garrity and producing.

Is Night Has Fallen happening?

Night Has Fallen, the fourth Has Fallen film, has completed production with Gerard Butler returning as Mike Banning. A release date has not been announced.

What is Gerard Butler's next movie?

Empire City, a hostage-crisis action thriller directed by Michael Matthews, is in post-production. The next confirmed dated release is How to Train Your Dragon 2 in 2027.

Will Gerard Butler be in How to Train Your Dragon 2?

Yes. Gerard Butler returns as Stoick the Vast in the 2027 sequel, which is filming now.
