// CAREER PERIOD // 2010–PRESENT

Franchise Icon & G-BASE Productions (2010–Present)

Executive Summary: From 2010 to the present, Gerard Butler established himself as a powerhouse producer and franchise action icon. Co-founding G-BASE Productions with Alan Siegel, Butler spearheaded the $500-million Has Fallen trilogy as Mike Banning, voiced Stoick the Vast in the How to Train Your Dragon franchise, and delivered acclaimed survival thrillers including Greenland (2020) and Plane (2023).

The Has Fallen Blockbuster Trilogy

Launching with Olympus Has Fallen (2013) directed by Antoine Fuqua, Butler created a modern action benchmark as Secret Service Agent Mike Banning. The series expanded with London Has Fallen (2016) and Angel Has Fallen (2019), consistently delivering gritty, hard-hitting theatrical thrills.

Stoick the Vast in How to Train Your Dragon

Butler brought heart, booming humor, and Viking gravitas to Stoick the Vast throughout DreamWorks Animation's beloved How to Train Your Dragon trilogy (2010, 2014, 2019). His immense connection with the character led to him being the only original cast member invited to reprise the role in the 2025 live-action feature.

G-BASE Productions & Original Survival Cinema

Through G-BASE, Butler took creative control of his slate, backing original director-driven thrillers. Standouts include the critically praised disaster drama Greenland (2020), high-concept aviation thriller Plane (2023), CIA operative saga Kandahar (2023), and heist series Den of Thieves.

Global Philanthropy with Mary's Meals

Throughout his modern career, Butler has dedicated his voice and resources to Mary's Meals, serving as Global Ambassador and visiting school feeding programs in Malawi, Liberia, and Haiti, helping the organization feed over 2.4 million hungry children every school day.
