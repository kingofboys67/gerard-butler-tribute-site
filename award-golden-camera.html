Golden Camera Award: Best International Actor (2016)

Executive Summary: In February 2016, Gerard Butler received Germany's prestigious Goldene Kamera (Golden Camera) Award for Best International Actor in Hamburg, Germany. The jury celebrated Butler's versatile three-decade career, exceptional dramatic range, and charismatic international screen presence across action, drama, and comedy.

Prestigious European Film Honor

The Goldene Kamera is Germany's most prominent television and film prize, presented annually since 1965. Previous international recipients include Clint Eastwood, Robert De Niro, and Colin Farrell. Butler was presented the award during a nationally televised gala at the Hamburg Messe.

Celebrating Dramatic & Cinematic Versatility

The jury citation praised Butler's remarkable capacity to transition effortlessly between visceral action blockbusters like Olympus Has Fallen, heartfelt romantic dramas like P.S. I Love You, and classical stage adaptations like The Phantom of the Opera.

Butler's Acceptance Speech

Accepting the iconic golden camera trophy, Butler delivered an emotional speech honoring the storytelling tradition of cinema, his Scottish roots, and his gratitude to international film audiences who have supported his cinematic journey over three decades.
