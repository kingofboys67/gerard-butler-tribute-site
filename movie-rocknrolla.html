Rocknrolla

Overview: RocknRolla (2008), directed by Guy Ritchie, is a sharp-witted London crime caper featuring Gerard Butler as street-smart hustler "One Two". Leading the Wild Bunch crew through property fraud and stolen Russian millions, Butler delivered one of his most stylish comedic-action turns.
