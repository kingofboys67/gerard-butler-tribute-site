// CAREER TIMELINE

From Glasgow law to Hollywood

  • 1992–1996 — Studies law at the University of Glasgow and trains at the Royal Conservatoire of Scotland; works briefly in a Glasgow law firm.
  • 1997 — Screen debut in Mrs Brown, followed by the Bond film Tomorrow Never Dies.
  • 2000–2001 — Leads as Count Dracula in Dracula 2000 and Attila the Hun in the miniseries Attila.
  • 2004 — Plays Erik in Joel Schumacher's The Phantom of the Opera.
  • 2006 — 300 makes him a global star; wins the MTV Movie Award for Best Fight in 2007.
  • 2010 — Begins voicing Stoick the Vast in the How to Train Your Dragon franchise.
  • 2013–2019 — Plays Secret Service agent Mike Banning across Olympus, London and Angel Has Fallen.
  • 2018 — Launches the Den of Thieves franchise as Big Nick O'Brien.
  • 2020–2023 — Leads Greenland, Copshop, Last Seen Alive, Plane and Kandahar.
  • 2025 — Reprises Stoick in the live-action How to Train Your Dragon, returns in Den of Thieves 2: Pantera, appears in In the Hand of Dante.
  • 2026 — Returns as John Garrity in Greenland 2: Migration (released 9 January 2026), which he also produced.

// QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions about Gerard Butler

How old is Gerard Butler?

Gerard Butler was born on 13 November 1969 in Paisley, Scotland, which makes him 56 years old as of October 2026.

What is Gerard Butler's full name?

His full name is Gerard James Butler. He is the youngest of three children born to Margaret and Edward Butler.

Where was Gerard Butler born?

He was born in Paisley, Renfrewshire, Scotland, and grew up in Paisley before his family moved to Montreal, Canada, for a period in his teens.

What was Gerard Butler's first film?

His screen debut was in Mrs Brown (1997), followed that year by a small role in the James Bond film Tomorrow Never Dies.

What is Gerard Butler best known for?

He is best known for 300 (2006), the Has Fallen trilogy, the How to Train Your Dragon films, The Phantom of the Opera (2004), Law Abiding Citizen, Den of Thieves and Greenland.

Did Gerard Butler study law?

Yes. He studied law at the University of Glasgow and served as president of its law society before training at the Royal Conservatoire of Scotland and turning to acting full time.

Is Gerard Butler married?

Gerard Butler is not married and has no publicly known children. He has consistently kept his private life out of the press.

What production company does Gerard Butler own?

He co-founded G-BASE Film Production in 2008 with his manager Alan Siegel. Its credits include Law Abiding Citizen, Olympus Has Fallen, Greenland, Plane and Greenland 2: Migration.

How tall is Gerard Butler?

Gerard Butler is widely reported to be 6 feet 2 inches (1.88 m) tall.

What charity does Gerard Butler support?

He is a long-standing Global Ambassador for Mary's Meals, the Scottish charity that provides school meals to more than 2.4 million children, and he supports disaster relief through Direct Relief.
