Machine Gun Preacher

Overview: Machine Gun Preacher (2011), directed by Marc Forster, stars Gerard Butler in the inspiring true story of Sam Childers, a former criminal who found faith and dedicated his life to rescuing thousands of war-affected children from militia violence in South Sudan.
